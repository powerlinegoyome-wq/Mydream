03 — Capabilities

What each submission includes.

01

Dual Placement

Every design ships with a small front chest mark and a full illustrated back graphic — the two placements Merch on Demand buyers expect from a heritage tee.

02

Original Brand Systems

Each piece is a self-contained identity: wordmark, tagline, founding date, and a repeatable crest — not a one-off graphic.

03

Print-Ready Files

Transparent PNG artwork at print resolution, separated from mockup backgrounds, ready for upload against Amazon's placement templates.

  • Research-grounded motifs — kilim borders, rune alphabets, kanji lockups, and heraldry are used with attention to the source culture, not generic "tribal" filler.
  • Cross-niche range — Anatolian folk art, Norse, Greco-Roman, and multiple distinct Japanese sub-niches (samurai, kitsune, koi, dragon) in one portfolio.
  • Consistent production system — a shared front/back/label structure across every design, so a buyer recognizes the "house style" across the catalogue.
  • Mockup presentation — each design is shown in a themed environment (gothic ruin, Spartan camp) suited to its lineage, for clean marketplace thumbnails.
